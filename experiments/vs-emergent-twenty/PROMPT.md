Build a marketing landing page for my product, Twenty.

**What it is:** Twenty is the #1 open-source CRM. It gives technical teams the
building blocks for a custom CRM that meets complex business needs and quickly
adapts as the business evolves: the CRM you build, ship, and version like the rest
of your stack. Objects, fields, views, workflows and AI agents are defined as code
with the Twenty CLI and SDK and published to a workspace; changes go through
version control like any other code. It includes all the tools of a good CRM:
custom data model, Kanban views, custom workflows, sorts and filters, email and
calendar sync, and live dashboards, plus AI agents and chats. Sign up at
twenty.com and spin up a workspace in under a minute with no infrastructure to
manage, or self-host it with Docker Compose.

**Who it is for:** technical teams at growing companies whose sales and operations
have outgrown an off-the-shelf CRM, and who want to customise their CRM in code
rather than in settings screens.

**Tone:** confident, precise and developer-friendly. Plain statements, no hype.

**Main button:** Get started. **Second button:** Self-host with Docker.

**Design references** (learn layout, pacing and craft from these, but don't copy
their branding): https://attio.com/ (the main one), https://linear.app/,
https://supabase.com/

**Rules:**
