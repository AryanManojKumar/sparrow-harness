# Blueprint: hero
**Order:** 1 · **File:** `src/components/sections/Hero.tsx`

Purpose: state what the product is and who it's for, in one screen, and offer the primary action.

Slots: `eyebrow` · `headline` · `subhead` · `primary_cta` · `secondary_cta` · `product_image`

Structure: full-width section on page ground. Content left-aligned in the container, product
image to the right, bleeding off the container's right edge on desktop and stacking below on
mobile. Headline uses the `display` step and is the only element on the page that does.
