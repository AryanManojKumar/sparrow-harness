# Blueprint: testimonial
**File:** `src/components/sections/Testimonial.tsx`

Purpose: Provide one compact, credible customer proof point showing that the agent harness made code-agent output materially more reviewable or adoptable at team scale.

Slots: customer_logo

Structure: Use a single testimonial block containing one short first-person quote, with one concrete measurable outcome or before-and-after figure embedded in the quote. Place a separate attribution line beneath it with the customer's name, role, and company; the customer logo is the only supporting image slot and is optional if no approved asset exists. Keep the block as a standalone single-column unit on desktop and preserve that hierarchy on mobile without adding a testimonial wall, carousel, CTA, feature list, or product demonstration that would duplicate the surrounding sections.
