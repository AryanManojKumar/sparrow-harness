# Blueprint: testimonial
**File:** `src/components/sections/Testimonial.tsx`

Purpose: Provide skeptical engineering leaders with compact, attributable evidence that adoption improved materially after using the agent harness.

Slots: quote · speaker_name · speaker_role · engineering_context · company_name

Structure: Use one compact testimonial block led by the customer quote, with the quantified before-and-after outcome retained verbatim or stated explicitly as adoption moving from single digits to over 80%. Place one attribution beneath it containing the speaker name, role, company, and engineering context so the evidence is identifiable rather than anonymous. On mobile, keep the quote and attribution as one sequential stack without introducing additional cards or testimonial variants. Do not include a broad trust headline, logo wall, feature explanation, numbered markers, or CTA, because adjacent sections handle product detail, proof through features, and conversion.
